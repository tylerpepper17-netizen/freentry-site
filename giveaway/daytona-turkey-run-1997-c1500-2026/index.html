One letter ≈ $5 of tickets

The free route yields 1 ticket, and a ticket sells for $5. Under F.S. 849.0935(4)(d) a free ticket and a bought one are the same chance, so the comparison is exact rather than approximate.

A charity raffle, not a sweepstakes

This is a Florida charity drawing held under F.S. 849.0935. Two parts of that statute change the maths. Subsection (4)(b) makes it unlawful to require a payment or donation to enter — the free route is compelled, not a courtesy. Subsection (4)(d) makes it unlawful to “discriminate in any manner between entrants who gave contributions to the organization and those who did not,” so a mailed-for ticket and a bought ticket are the same chance.
